Joel Armstrong & Gobo released "Still Here" (HMDIGITAL007, 2006) on Hunya Munya's digital sublabel: a ten-minute breaks track that remains the duo's only documented release together. The project's full backstory and member identities are not part of the public record.

[Joel Armstrong](/artists/joel-armstrong) returned to the label on his own in 2007 with a remix of Boom Jinx's *Flicker*.
