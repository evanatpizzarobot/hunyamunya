Onirika is the alias of Italian DJ and producer Roberta Galvini, one of the founders of LDU
Records, who also served as A&R for Rax Musik.

She contributed two tracks, *Running* and *Zufriedene*, to the HMDIGITAL015 *International
Soundscapes EP* on Hunya Munya Digital in 2008.
