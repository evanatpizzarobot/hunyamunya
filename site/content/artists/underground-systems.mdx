Underground Systems ran from 1995 to 2019, a stretch of more than two decades that covers the shift from the early web through the whole of the download era. The Hunya Munya release sits close to the middle of it.

That release was "IFT" (HMDIGITAL008, 2006), on Hunya Munya's digital sublabel: a three-track digital set pairing the original with the companion piece "Rastafari" and a "Fairy Tale Mix" by Hippi. The record moves through breaks, minimal, and tech-house.
