Joel Armstrong is a Canadian progressive house and breaks producer whose most productive stretch ran from the middle of the 2000s into the turn of the decade. *Serenity* landed on Proton Music in 2005, *Silver Moon* on France's Babylon Records in 2006 with a Derek Howell remix on the flip, and *The Jitters* on Ascension Records Digital in 2007. He also remixed Markus Schulz's *Ibiza '06* that same year. By 2008 he had three 12"s out on Dot Dot Records, among them *The Vancity EP*, plus *El Squelcho* on Nightshade and *Runaway Reject* with Derek Kehler on Proton Particles. He is a member of the Peg City Allstars.

Two of those years touch Hunya Munya. [*Still Here*](/catalog/hmdigital007-still-here) (HMDIGITAL007, 2006) is his ten-minute breaks track with Gobo for the label's digital sublabel. The other is his remix of Boom Jinx's [*Flicker*](/catalog/hmr006-flicker), cut in the same window and arriving on the digital edition of HMR006 rather than on the 2007 12".
